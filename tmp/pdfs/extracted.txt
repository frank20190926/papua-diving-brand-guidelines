05 / VOICE & TONE




WARM. INFORMED.
SPECIFIC.
Speak as a thoughtful host who knows the place. Invite discovery, share useful knowledge
and give conservation claims context.




INVITE, DO NOT OVERSELL


SAY          Explore Raja Ampat with the people who know its reefs.



AVOID        The ultimate untouched paradise.




MAKE RESPONSIBILITY TANGIBLE


SAY          Meet the guides and learn how to care for the reef during your stay.



AVOID        The world's most sustainable resort.




RESPECT LIVING CULTURE


SAY          Papuan people, knowledge and traditions are part of the story.



AVOID        Exotic tribes and primitive island life.




Sorido Bay: calm, attentive, refined. Kri Eco: direct, resourceful, close to nature. Both: welcoming, educational and rooted
in place. Check names, figures, awards and environmental claims before release.

CONSISTENCY
IS A PRACTICE.

LOGO                     DO Keep the supplied master, 1x clearance and minimum sizes.


                         AVOID Recolour, stretch, redraw, reverse or crowd the mark.




COLOUR                   DO Use the specified master colours and resort emphasis.


                         AVOID Add new blues, overuse accents or guess print conversions.

                         AVOID Bring back Karla, fake bold Tenor or use African as body text.
