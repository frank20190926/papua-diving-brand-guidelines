01 / Brand architecture

Master brand over two sub-brands

Master brand

Papua Diving Resorts

Carries the bird glyph, drawn from Southwest Papuan bark-cloth painting. Logo is supplied artwork and never redrawn.

Sub-brand · Upscale Boutique

Sorido Bay Resort

Linen-led with Papua Brown and Ochre Clay. Refined, design-led hospitality.

Sub-brand · Authentic Eco-Pioneer

Kri Eco Resort

Sago Leaf leads; Sandstone and Charcoal ground it. Honest, hands-on, close to nature.
